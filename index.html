The Stay

Room for the whole crew

Tucked among the cedars and sugar pines of Arnold, The Hoopa Hangout is built for family reunions, ski weekends and friends' getaways. Four bedrooms and three full bathrooms mean everyone gets their own space, and the open living areas bring you back together for game nights, big dinners and slow mornings with coffee.

Spend the day hiking among giant sequoias, skiing at Bear Valley or paddling a mountain lake, then come home to a warm, well-stocked cabin.

The Area

Arnold & the Ebbetts Pass corridor

Calaveras Big Trees

Walk among ancient giant sequoias at Calaveras Big Trees State Park, just a short drive up Highway 4.

Bear Valley

Skiing and snowboarding in winter, mountain biking and alpine lakes in summer.

Lakes & Rivers

Swim, paddle and fish at White Pines Lake, Lake Alpine and the Stanislaus River.

Murphys

Wine tasting, restaurants and Gold Rush history on Main Street in Murphys.

Caves & Caverns

Tour Moaning Cavern and Mercer Caverns, or zipline over the canyon.

Town of Arnold

Groceries, coffee, pizza and local shops are just minutes from the cabin.

FAQ

Good to know

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 4:00 PM and check-out is at 10:00 AM. Self check-in details are sent before your arrival.

Are pets allowed?

Please contact us about pets before booking.

Do I need chains or 4WD in winter?

Arnold gets real snow. From late fall through spring, carry chains or drive a 4WD/AWD vehicle with snow tires, and check Caltrans road conditions for Highway 4.

Why book direct instead of Airbnb or VRBO?

Booking direct skips the platform service fees, and you deal with us directly. Payments are processed securely through OwnerRez.

What is the cancellation policy?

The full cancellation policy is shown during checkout before you pay.

Are events or parties allowed?

No parties or events. Calaveras County quiet hours are observed.
